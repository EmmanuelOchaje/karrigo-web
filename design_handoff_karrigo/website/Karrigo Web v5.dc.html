Visit help →
Do I need to create an account?+

No. You can order with just a phone number. We text you a link to follow the order, and you can make an account later if you want your addresses saved.

What if I do not have a street address?+

Most people here do not use one. Give us a landmark — a school gate, a church, a filling station — and the area. Riders find it the same way you would tell a friend.

How much is delivery?+

It depends on how far the kitchen is from you, so it is shown per kitchen before you order. It is never a surprise at checkout.

Can I pay cash?+

Yes, cash to the rider when the food reaches you. Card and bank transfer also work.

Which areas do you deliver to?+

Seven areas around Makurdi today. We open a new area only when we have enough riders to serve it properly, rather than taking orders we cannot deliver.

Do I need a computer?+

No, a phone is enough.

What if something is out of stock?+

Mark it in the app. The customer chooses a replacement or gets a refund.

How are weighed items priced?+

You enter the actual weight. The customer pays for exactly that.

When do I get paid?+

Every week, straight to your bank account.

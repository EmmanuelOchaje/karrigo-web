D6 · Dropped from the earlier design
Stores as a separate thing

The Stores page, its own onboarding (Pending → Catalogue review → Live), CAC checks and the coverage decision. There is one kitchens list, one detail page and one approval step. “Groceries” in the sidebar is a filtered view of Kitchens, and “Flagged items” sits beneath it. The rest of the ops nav, the theme switch and the shift and signed-in cards are unchanged from the admin app.

Store performance metrics

Fill rate, substitution rate, average picking time and out-of-stock counts. There are no stock counts and no picking flow, so none of these exist.

Catalogue moderation actions

“Set ₦600”, “Set unit”, “Merge”, “Use library photo”, “Ask store”, “Send back to store” and the CSV import. Admins flag a product with a note; the owner fixes it.

Grocery issue types

Low fill rate, picking too slow, unanswered substitution and large order with no rider. None of the underlying features exist. Issues stays as it is for kitchens and riders.

Estimate vs final money

Card holds, released amounts and substitution refunds. A customer pays for what the store accepts, so there is one amount per order.

The Friday payout run

Payouts are triggered by a super admin per kitchen, and a missing commission rate blocks that row.

What the store can do

Accept or reject an incoming order.
Remove items it hasn’t got, which lowers what the customer is charged.
Pack against a tick list and mark the order ready.
Hand the bags to a rider and confirm.
Open or close the store to new orders.

Stripped out

Catalogue review and item approval.
Substitution suggestions and customer chat.
Weighed items and price adjustments.
Bag counts, weights and the rider app.
Prep estimates, slots and large-order handling.

Still open

Whether a rejected order refunds automatically or waits on admin, and whether the store can edit quantities as well as remove lines. Both need a backend answer.

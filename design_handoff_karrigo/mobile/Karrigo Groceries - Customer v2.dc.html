Groceries · customer app

Rebuilt to the backend note of 6 October. A store is a kitchen that serves groceries, a customer holds one cart at a time, products are a name and a price, and the store removes anything it hasn’t got when it accepts. Substitutions, weights, estimates, holds, cash, slots and large orders are gone.

Clickable. Start on Home, open Makurdi Mega Store, add items, check out, then watch the store accept and trim the order. The jump list skips to any screen.
